  <div class="home-lead__text" markdown="1">

I am a research associate at the [Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/). I work with the empirical macro group, primarily on macro-labor topics using a micro-data toolkit.

I hold a B.A. in Economics and Mathematics from the University of Virginia (2022). I am completing an M.S. in Economics at Drexel University part-time (finishing Dec 2026) while working full-time at the Fed.

In my (very limited) free hours, I play viola in the South Jersey Pops symphony!

  <!-- ================= RESEARCH ================= -->
  <section class="tab-panel is-active" id="tab-research" role="tabpanel">
    <p>My work is primarily microeconomic in data and method. At the Fed, I work in the Empirical Macro section of the Research Department, supporting the research of Senior Economic Advisor and Economist Shigeru Fujita, and I also spend part of my time on the <a href="https://www.philadelphiafed.org/surveys-and-data/macroeconomic-data/prism-ii">PRISM-II DSGE model</a>. I have extensive experience with CPS Basic Monthly, CPS ASEC, and PSID data, as well as restricted-access Social Security data, the Health and Retirement Study, the American Community Survey, the Fujita&ndash;Ramey expanded Palmer data, and a range of macroeconomic time series.</p>

    <div class="section-head">Policy &amp; non-technical writing</div>
    <div class="pub-list">
      <div class="pub">
        <div class="pub__title"><a href="https://www.philadelphiafed.org/the-economy/nonworking-parents-or-hungry-children">Nonworking Parents or Hungry Children? The Child Tax Credit and Work Incentives</a></div>
        <div class="pub__meta">Economic Insights, Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia (coauthored)</div>
        <p class="pub__abstract">A non-technical article examining the trade-offs between work incentives and child poverty in the design of the Child Tax Credit.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="section-head">Works in progress</div>
    <div class="pub-list">
      <div class="pub">
        <span class="pub__status">Work in progress</span>
        <div class="pub__title">The Intergenerational Transmission of Occupation Skill and Preference</div>
        <p class="pub__abstract">I study whether certain professions have become more or less "heritable" over time, estimating the task-based similarity between parent and child occupations and how it has evolved.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="pub">
        <span class="pub__status">Work in progress</span>
        <div class="pub__title">Immigration, Nonresponse, and Refusal in the Current Population Survey</div>
        <p class="pub__abstract">I estimate the degree to which undocumented immigration drives increased nonresponse &mdash; and specifically refusal &mdash; in the CPS, and examine how rising refusal rates may bias employment statistics. I hope to produce a reweighting procedure that better reconciles the household and payroll surveys.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="pub">
        <span class="pub__status">Work in progress</span>
        <div class="pub__title">School Facilities Quality and Neighborhood Outcomes in Philadelphia</div>
        <p class="pub__abstract">Using a bespoke dataset of facilities condition assessments from the School District of Philadelphia, I explore relationships between facilities quality and demographic composition, enrollment flows, and attendance &mdash; and whether facilities decline is capitalized in catchment home values.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="section-head">Older work</div>
    <div class="pub-list">
      <div class="pub">
        <div class="pub__title">Access to Childcare Subsidies, Labor Supply, and Welfare Program Participation</div>
        <div class="pub__meta">Undergraduate thesis, University of Virginia</div>
        <p class="pub__abstract">I examine how accessible childcare alters the screening effects of welfare work requirements, using SNAP and the 2014 CCDBG reauthorization for identification.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="pub">
        <div class="pub__title"><a href="/files/perry_4880_paper%20copy.docx">A Longer Look at Merit Aid, Educational Attainment, and Post-Degree Migration Decisions</a></div>
        <div class="pub__meta">Recipient of the UVA Economics Department award for Best Public Policy Paper by an Undergraduate (2021)</div>
      </div>
    </div>
    <p style="margin-top:1.2em;">For additional undergraduate work, see the <a href="/archive/">archive</a>.</p>
  </section>

(function () {
  var buttons = document.querySelectorAll('.tab-btn');
  function activate(name) {
    document.querySelectorAll('.tab-btn').forEach(function (b) {
      b.classList.toggle('is-active', b.getAttribute('data-tab') === name);
    });
    document.querySelectorAll('.tab-panel').forEach(function (p) {
      p.classList.toggle('is-active', p.id === 'tab-' + name);
    });
  }
  buttons.forEach(function (btn) {
    btn.addEventListener('click', function () {
      var name = btn.getAttribute('data-tab');
      activate(name);
      if (history.replaceState) history.replaceState(null, '', '#' + name);
    });
  });
  var hash = (location.hash || '').replace('#', '');
  if (hash && document.querySelector('.tab-btn[data-tab="' + hash + '"]')) activate(hash);
})();
</script>
